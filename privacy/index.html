Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-11

This policy covers Does It Run Publisher ("the app"), a private tool used by a single person, the owner of the "Does It Run?" brand, and this website. The app is used only to publish the owner's own videos to the owner's own YouTube channel (@doesitrunhq) and the owner's own Instagram account (@doesitrunhq). It is not offered to the public and no one else signs in to it.

What the app accesses

  • YouTube (Google OAuth scopes youtube.upload and youtube.readonly): to upload our videos as YouTube Shorts and to read the owner's own channel and video statistics (views, likes, comments counts) so we can see what works.
  • Instagram (content publish permission through the official Instagram API): to publish our own Reels to the owner's own account and read the status of those posts.

What we do not do

  • We do not collect, store or process personal data of third parties, viewers, commenters or subscribers.
  • We do not sell, rent or share any data, and we do not use it for advertising.
  • We do not transfer data to third parties; the app talks only to Google and Instagram APIs.

Where tokens are stored

OAuth access and refresh tokens are stored locally, on the owner's own computer, in a file that is not published or committed to any repository. They are never sent anywhere except to Google and Instagram when calling their APIs. The only data kept is our own videos' metadata and public statistics, also stored locally.

Google API Services User Data Policy / Limited Use

Does It Run Publisher's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Specifically: data from Google APIs is used only to provide and improve the single feature of publishing and monitoring the owner's own videos; it is not transferred to others except as needed to provide that feature or to comply with law; it is not used for advertising; and no human reads it other than the owner.

How to revoke access

  • Google: remove the app at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Instagram: remove the app in your Instagram settings under Apps and websites (Settings, Website permissions).

After revoking, delete the local token file to remove stored credentials.

This website

The site is static and hosted on GitHub Pages. We run no analytics and set no cookies. GitHub may log standard server requests under its own privacy statement.
